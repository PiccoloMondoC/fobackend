




# Sagrenti bills for merchant value delivered, not for user actions

**Articulate in conversation:**
> Sagrenti doesn't charge merchants for consumer clicks. It charges for Anticipation Intelligence. Consumer engagements are simply the measurable units through which that intelligence is delivered.

**Articulate the engineering doctrine behind it**
>A billable event represents the delivery of measurable merchant value, not merely the occurrence of a consumer action. Consumer engagements are recorded because they generate Anticipation Intelligence. Fee calculations determine whether, when, and how that delivered value becomes chargeable.

**Sagrenti's business model**
>Sagrenti monetizes anticipation, not transactions.

# For example
**Traditional platforms say:**

>"We helped complete a sale. Pay us."

**Sagrenti says:**

> "We helped you understand, cultivate, and measure demand before the sale ever existed. That's the value we provide."
