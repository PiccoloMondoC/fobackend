### Future Offering
A merchant's business object concerning a product or service yet to be released into the market at a future date and time. Future Offering is the merchant's asset.

Examples:

* A new iPhone launch
* New EV
* AI SaaS platform
* Music festival
* Hotel opening
* Restaurant launch


### Launch Intelligence
Sagrenti's service from observing the Future Offering. Launch Intelligence is Sagrenti's product.

For example:

* anticipation trends
* geographic interest
* demographic interest
* watch growth
* reservation demand
* preorder intent
* launch readiness
* demand forecasting
* engagement analytics

A merchant doesn't buy a Future Offering from Sagrenti.

They already own the Future Offering.

They buy **intelligence about it**.

### My Radar
Consumer engagement. Engagement actions may include:

* watch

* early_access
* beta
