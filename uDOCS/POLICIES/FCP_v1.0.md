# Future Commerce Principles (FCP) v1.0

## 1. Purpose

This document defines the core principles that govern how Sagrenti handles consumer engagement with Future Offerings.

Its purpose is to keep Sagrenti focused on anticipation intelligence while allowing merchants to remain responsible for commerce.

---

## 2. The Core Principle

**Anticipation belongs to Sagrenti. Commerce belongs to the merchant.**

Sagrenti exists to discover Future Offerings, measure consumer anticipation, and connect interested consumers with merchants.

Merchants remain responsible for selling their products and services.

---

## 3. The Universal Consumer Action

Every Future Offering includes a **Watch** action.

When a consumer clicks **Watch**, the Future Offering is automatically added to the consumer's **Radar**.

Watching a Future Offering is the foundation of all future consumer engagement.

---

## 4. Consumer Engagement

Once a Future Offering is on the consumer's Radar, the consumer may express stronger levels of interest through engagement options such as:

* Waitlist
* Early Access Request
* Beta Application
* Reservation Interest
* Preorder Intent

Each engagement represents a deeper level of consumer intent.

These engagements are managed by Sagrenti.

Because these engagements already imply ongoing communication, there is no separate **Notify Me** feature.

---

## 5. Staying Inside Sagrenti

As long as the consumer is expressing interest, everything remains inside Sagrenti.

Sagrenti manages:

* Watch
* Radar
* Consumer engagement
* Notifications
* Anticipation analytics
* Merchant intelligence

During this phase, consumer contact information does not need to be shared with the merchant.

---

## 6. Merchant Handoff

Some engagement options eventually require direct interaction with the merchant.

Examples include:

* Beta participants being accepted
* Reservations being confirmed
* Preorders being completed

When that point is reached, Sagrenti hands the consumer to the merchant.

The merchant then becomes responsible for:

* Customer information
* Checkout
* Payment
* Refunds
* Returns
* Fulfillment
* Customer support

---

## 7. What Sagrenti Is Not

Sagrenti is not a checkout platform.

Sagrenti is not the merchant of record.

Sagrenti does not process payments for Future Commerce.

Sagrenti measures anticipation and facilitates discovery.

Merchants complete commercial transactions.

---

## 8. Architectural Guideline

A simple test should guide every Future Commerce feature:

**Does this help measure or manage anticipation?**

If yes, it belongs in Sagrenti.

**Does this complete a commercial transaction?**

If yes, it belongs to the merchant.

---

## 9. Summary

Future Commerce is built on a simple separation of responsibilities.

**Sagrenti owns anticipation.**

**Merchants own commerce.**

This principle keeps Sagrenti focused on its unique value: helping consumers discover what matters next while giving merchants measurable insight into consumer anticipation before products and services become generally available.
