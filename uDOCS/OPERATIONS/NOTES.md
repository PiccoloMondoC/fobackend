
### CREDIT APPLICATIONS

>Sagrenti must never apply more platform credit to a fee calculation than the merchant actually owes.

**policy decides what the platform is allowed or configured to do; invariants make it impossible for the platform to do something financially nonsensical even when things go wrong.**

## Currency
Merchant domicile must not determine invoice currency. Invoice currency is established from the relevant billing context and becomes immutable once the invoice is issued.


**engineering defines what an invoice can safely represent; Administration decides the commercial terms.**
