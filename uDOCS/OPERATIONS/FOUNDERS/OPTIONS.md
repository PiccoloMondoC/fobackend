


### Overall Option Pool Size
* Seed Stage: 10% to 15% 
* Early/Foundational: Can scale up to 20%

### Individual Grant Sizes by Role
* C-Suite / Co-founder level (non-founding): 1.0% to 5.0%
* VP / Head of Department: 0.5% to 2.0%
* Director / Senior Manager: 0.4% to 1.0%
* Early Engineer / Hire #1–#5: 1.0% to 3.0% (decreasing as the team grows)
* Mid-Level Manager: 0.2% to 0.5%
* Junior / Entry-Level Employee: 0.05% to 0.2%

### Key Structuring Rules
* Vesting: 4-year vesting schedule with a 1-year cliff (meaning no options vest if the employee leaves before 12 months).

Hiring Roadmap: Calculate the exact employee pool size based on a realistic hiring projection for the next 18 to 24 months rather than picking an arbitrary number.
